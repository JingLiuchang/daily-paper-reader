## 摘要
由流匹配和扩散驱动的连续生成式检索器在固定预算下于条目嵌入空间中采样多个查询，以进行近似最近邻（ANN）搜索。然而，现有生成式检索器在未协调采样时可能遭遇检索重叠，而通过显式多样性引导来拓宽候选集又可能因缺乏对额外条目价值的直接反馈而引发相关性漂移。我们提出 CACO（Coverage-Aware Coordinated Optimization，覆盖感知协同优化），这是一个自监督后训练框架，可协调来自冻结的流匹配与扩散检索器的查询，以近乎可忽略的延迟开销实现互补的条目覆盖。CACO 将采样参考查询上的经验检索频率作为相关性代理，构建无标签覆盖目标，在离线实验中提升了全部 30 个骨干-数据集设置的召回率。为避免推理时采样参考查询的开销，CACO 采用协调查询调整网络，结合查询间双向注意力与门控相对方向，通过有界切空间投影和球面回缩来更新查询。训练时，CACO 施加切向对偶扰动与反事实单查询替换，以度量每个查询的边际增益，从而在不可微的 ANN 搜索上得到平滑集合覆盖的无偏梯度估计器。在八个公开基准和一个内部 24 分区流式工业数据集上，CACO 相比最强基线将 Recall@100 和 Recall@400 分别最高提升 23.7% 和 23.9%，将查询间重叠降低 53.3%-64.9%，且仅增加 0.5%-3.0% 的推理延迟。

## Abstract
Continuous generative retrievers powered by flow matching and diffusion sample multiple queries in item embedding space for approximate nearest-neighbor (ANN) search under fixed budgets. However, existing generative retrievers can suffer from retrieval overlap under uncoordinated sampling, whereas explicit diversity guidance to broaden the candidate set may induce relevance drift as it lacks direct feedback on the value of additional items. We introduce CACO (Coverage-Aware Coordinated Optimization), a self-supervised post-training framework coordinating queries from frozen flow-matching and diffusion retrievers toward complementary item coverage with negligible latency overhead. Using empirical retrieval frequencies across sampled reference queries as a relevance proxy, CACO formulates a label-free coverage objective that improves recall across all 30 backbone-dataset settings in offline experiments. To avoid the overhead of sampling reference queries at inference, CACO employs a coordinated query adjustment network combining inter-query bidirectional attention with gated relative directions, updating queries via bounded tangent-space projection and spherical retraction. During training, CACO applies tangential antithetic perturbations and counterfactual single-query replacement to measure each query's marginal gain, yielding an unbiased gradient estimator of smoothed set coverage across non-differentiable ANN search. On eight public benchmarks and an in-house 24-partition streaming industrial dataset, CACO improves Recall@100 and Recall@400 by up to 23.7% and 23.9% over the strongest baseline, cuts inter-query overlap by 53.3%-64.9%, and adds only 0.5%-3.0% inference latency.
