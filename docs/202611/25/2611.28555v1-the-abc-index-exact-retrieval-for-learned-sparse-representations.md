## 摘要
在学习稀疏检索（LSR）中，查询包含数十个带有学习权重的词项，经典动态剪枝被认为在此类查询上会失效，而 Seismic 和 HNSW 等近似索引被认为比精确索引更快。我们以 abc 挑战这一观点；abc 是一个仅由标准信息检索技术构建的精确索引：逐词项（TAAT）评估、Block-Max MaxScore、二进制打包和文档 ID 重排序。这些技术以新的设计组合在一起，并针对现代 CPU 进行了工程优化。在由四种 LSR 模型编码的 MS MARCO 上，abc 索引在每一个 k 值下都是最快的精确索引：比 Qdrant 的精确搜索快 6.5–84 倍，比 Lucene 的精确搜索快 13–55 倍，并且以小 5–7 倍的索引比 BMP 快 2.9–18 倍。与近似索引相比，它占用的空间少 1.5–3.6 倍，且其延迟随 k 增长得慢得多：在 k=10 时，近似索引最多快 4.3 倍，但在 k=1000 时，abc 反超它们，最多快 7.4 倍。精确性还带来额外好处：无需调参。

## Abstract
In learned sparse retrieval (LSR), queries have tens of terms with learned weights, on which classic dynamic pruning is believed to fail, and approximate indexes such as Seismic and HNSW are considered faster than exact ones. We challenge this view with abc, an exact index built only from standard IR techniques: term-at-a-time (TAAT) evaluation, Block-Max MaxScore, binary packing and doc-id reordering. They are combined in a new design and engineered for modern CPUs. On MS MARCO encoded with four LSR models, the abc index is the fastest exact index at every k: 6.5-84x faster than the exact search of Qdrant, 13-55x faster than that of Lucene, and 2.9-18x faster than BMP with an index 5-7x smaller. Against approximate indexes, it takes 1.5-3.6x less space, and its latency grows much more slowly with k: they are up to 4.3x faster at k=10, but at k=1000 it overtakes them, by up to 7.4x. Exactness comes with an extra benefit: there are no parameters to tune.
