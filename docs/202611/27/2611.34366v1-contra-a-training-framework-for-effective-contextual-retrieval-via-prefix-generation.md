## 摘要
数据管理系统组织大规模文本集合，以支持查找相关研究和提取合同条款等任务。系统通过将长文档切分为可独立索引的块来使其可搜索。分块可能使文本与其识别性细节相分离。索引面临的挑战在于：在保持每个块可独立搜索的同时，保留查找和排序该块所需的上下文。现有方法要么将该上下文编码到块向量中，要么将其作为文本前缀附加到块上。仅编码在向量中的上下文无法被文本重排器使用。提示生成的前缀可同时服务于向量搜索和重排，但在索引时生成成本高，且性能可能不如上下文编码器。我们提出 ConTra（Context That Travels），一个用于训练轻量级前缀生成器的强化学习框架。其奖励函数将来自冻结嵌入模型和重排模型的反馈与事实性验证和长度控制相结合。ConTra 在索引构建期间为每个块生成一个前缀，并存储增强后的文本及其嵌入，以便跨查询复用。为了评估跨工作负载以及受干扰情况下的上下文检索，我们引入 RealContextBench，一个包含五个应用集合和三种受控条件的基准，这些条件可隔离干扰性文本的影响。在 ConTEB 和 RealContextBench 上，ConTra-2B 优于使用 Anthropic 方法的提示式 9B 基线以及 PPLX-4B 上下文编码器，在它们最佳总体得分上最高领先 3.15 分，同时索引短文档的速度分别达到这些基线的 7.74 倍和 2.15 倍。仅在 ConTEB 上进行后训练后，它在重排后于 RealContextBench 上领先于独立方法，并且相比提示式 2B 基线，对随机干扰项和主题相似干扰项更不敏感。代码、数据集和训练好的模型可在 https://github.com/SStan1/ConTra 获取。

## Abstract
Data management systems organize large text collections for tasks such as finding relevant studies and extracting contract clauses. Systems make long documents searchable by splitting them into independently indexed chunks. Chunking can separate text from its identifying details. The indexing challenge is to keep each chunk independently searchable while preserving the context needed to find and rank it. Existing methods either encode that context in chunk vectors or attach it as a text prefix. Context encoded only in vectors is unavailable to text rerankers. Prompted prefixes serve both vector search and reranking, but are costly to generate at indexing time and can underperform contextual encoders. We propose ConTra (Context That Travels), a reinforcement learning framework for training lightweight prefix generators. Its reward combines feedback from frozen embedding and reranking models with factual verification and length control. ConTra generates one prefix per chunk during index construction and stores the augmented text and its embedding for reuse across queries. To evaluate contextual retrieval across workloads and under interference, we introduce RealContextBench, a benchmark with five application collections and three controlled conditions that isolate the effect of distracting text. On ConTEB and RealContextBench, ConTra-2B outperforms the prompted 9B baseline using Anthropic's method and the PPLX-4B contextual encoder, with a margin of up to 3.15 points over their best overall scores, while indexing short documents 7.74 times and 2.15 times as fast as those baselines, respectively. Post-trained only on ConTEB, it leads the standalone methods on RealContextBench after reranking and is less sensitive than the prompted 2B baseline to random and topically similar distractors. Code, datasets, and trained models are available at https://github.com/SStan1/ConTra.
