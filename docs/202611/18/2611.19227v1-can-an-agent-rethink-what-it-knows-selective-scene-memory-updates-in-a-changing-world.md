## 摘要
工厂、仓库和日常物理环境中的任务配置会随时间变化。因此，物理 AI 系统需要将新事实纳入场景记忆，同时保留关于设备、物体和运行约束的有效信息。我们研究对这一参数化语义记忆层的更新，并在有限更新预算下对纠正与保持进行联合评估。在当前事实数量和记忆参数数量固定的情况下，该协议结合了独立的单事实更新、预算轨迹、旧答案到新答案的诊断以及首次稳定成功。我们使用冻结的 4B 语言模型并在后层采用 LoRA，在四字段合成语义配置上评估七种准备条件和六种更新预算。在两种曝光量匹配的历史条件下，将预算从 8 次更新增加到 32 次更新，分别使联合成功率从 6.25% 提高到 94.79% 和 91.15%，而精确输出过时值的次数分别从 174/192 和 166/192 降至零。Constant32 与准备步骤相匹配，但在前 32 个事件中反复训练未变化内容，在 8 步时取得更高的早期联合成功率（53.65%）；在 32 步时达到 85.42%，低于两个变化历史条件。这些结果区分了小预算下的快速纠正与进一步训练后新事实和其他三个事实同时正确。对于两种变化历史，从第 16 步起，大多数案例在所有后续测量点仍保持联合正确。输出分析表明，短预算失败主要保留旧值；将新事实与其他当前事实一起重复训练会逐步替换这些过时答案。

## Abstract
Task configurations in factories, warehouses, and everyday physical environments change over time. Physical AI systems therefore need to incorporate new facts into scene memory while preserving valid information about equipment, objects, and operating constraints. We study updates to this parametric semantic-memory layer and present a joint evaluation of correction and preservation under finite update budgets. At fixed current fact count and memory parameter count, the protocol combines independent single-fact updates, budget trajectories, old-to-new answer diagnostics, and first stable success. Using a frozen 4B language model with late-layer LoRA, we evaluate seven preparation conditions and six update budgets on four-field synthetic semantic configurations. In two exposure-matched history conditions, increasing the budget from eight to 32 updates raises joint success from 6.25% to 94.79% and 91.15%, respectively, while exact obsolete-value outputs fall from 174/192 and 166/192 to zero. Constant32, which matches preparation steps but repeatedly trains on unchanged content during the first 32 events, achieves higher early joint success at eight steps (53.65%); at 32 steps, it reaches 85.42%, below both changing-history conditions. These results distinguish rapid correction under a small budget from simultaneous correctness of the new fact and the other three facts after further training. For both changing histories, most cases remain jointly correct at all subsequent measured points from step 16 onward. Output analysis shows that short-budget failures primarily retain the old value; repeated training on the new fact together with the other current facts progressively replaces these obsolete answers.
