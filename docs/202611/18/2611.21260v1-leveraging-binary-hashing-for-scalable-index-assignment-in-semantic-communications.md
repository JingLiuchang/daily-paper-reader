## 摘要
在数字信道上传输语义结构化的嵌入向量在比特率方面代价高昂，而传统向量量化方法在可扩展性、编码复杂度和索引分配方面存在局限。本文研究信息检索文献中的二值哈希技术，将其作为通用网络的语义索引策略。我们在二进制对称信道上的语义通信流水线中，研究随机局部敏感哈希（LSH）以及基于主成分分析（PCA）、典型相关分析（CCA）和迭代量化（ITQ）的变体。嵌入被压缩为二进制码，通过从共享表中进行汉明检索来重建，并在下游分类任务上评估。在 Massive Text Embedding Benchmark（MTEB）和 Banking77 数据集上的实验表明，数据相关哈希优于随机 LSH，而 ITQ 细化提高了对信道噪声的鲁棒性。在评估的方法中，CCA-ITQ 在测试的信道条件下取得了最佳分类准确率。这些结果表明，二值哈希是语义通信中基于量化的嵌入压缩的一种高效且可扩展的替代方案。

## Abstract
Transmitting semantically structured embedding vectors over digital channels is costly in bit rate, while conven- tional vector quantization approaches face limitations in scalabil- ity, encoding complexity, and index assignment. This paper inves- tigates binary hashing techniques from the information retrieval literature as semantic indexing strategies for general-purpose networks. We study random Locality-Sensitive Hashing (LSH) along with Principal Component Analysis (PCA)-, Canonical Correlation Analysis (CCA)-, and ITQ (Iterative Quantization)- based variants in a semantic communication pipeline over a Binary Symmetric Channel. Embeddings are compressed into binary codes, reconstructed through Hamming retrieval from a shared table, and evaluated on downstream classification. Ex- periments on the Massive Text Embedding Benchmark (MTEB) and the Banking77 dataset show that data-dependent hashing improves upon random LSH, while ITQ refinement increases robustness to channel noise. Among the evaluated methods, CCA- ITQ achieves the best classification accuracy across the tested channel conditions. These results indicate that binary hashing is an efficient and scalable alternative to quantization-based embedding compression for semantic communications.
