## 摘要
AI智能体正日益部署于具有社会影响的场景中，但现有评估难以充分保证其在多样化交互中始终按照预期特征一致地行事。我们提出一个用于衡量社会部署AI智能体行为一致性的评估框架。该框架沿五个可测量维度对智能体行为进行操作化，并采用一套标准化评估组套，覆盖六类具有社会显著性的交互场景，以评估行为区分度、一致性和漂移。受控人设配置作为实验锚点，使系统性检验成为可能：预期的行为差异是否在不同情境中持续存在，而非仅表现为风格变化。初步分析显示不同配置之间存在可测量的区分度，并提示行为画像与交互结果之间存在关联。通过将关注点从人设设计转向行为保障，这项工作为评估社会部署的AI智能体能否在用户与之相遇的各种情境中一致且可测量地按预期行事提供了基础。

## Abstract
AI agents are increasingly deployed in socially consequential settings, yet existing evaluations provide limited assurance that they behave consistently with their intended characteristics across diverse interactions. We introduce an evaluation framework for measuring the behavioral consistency of socially deployed AI agents. The framework operationalizes agent behavior along five measurable axes and uses a standardized evaluation battery spanning six socially salient interaction settings to assess behavioral differentiation, consistency, and drift. Controlled persona configurations serve as experimental anchors, enabling systematic testing of whether intended behavioral differences persist across contexts rather than appearing only as stylistic variation. Preliminary analyses demonstrate measurable differentiation across configurations and suggest associations between behavioral profiles and interaction outcomes. By shifting the focus from persona design to behavioral assurance, this work provides a foundation for evaluating whether socially deployed AI agents behave as intended, consistently and measurably, across the situations in which users encounter them.
